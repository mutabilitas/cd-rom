About

Madina Malahayati Chumaera is a [constellation of multitudes][25-year-old writer & researcher & translator & ... ] from Jakarta, Indonesia.

CONTACT LIGHT: the void inside and out was published by Gramedia Pustaka Utama in October 2017. Her self-published chapbook IF I'M NOT A FIRE THEN WHAT AM I, a collection of selected writings from 2015-2019, was released in June 2020.

Her areas of interest include: poetical science (& other ways to describe the intersection of the humanities & sciences); aphantasia (both in preparation for future postgraduate studies in cognitive science, and for her own metacognition); critical approaches to technology & AI; Indonesian literature & history; memory- and archive-keeping; & ...
